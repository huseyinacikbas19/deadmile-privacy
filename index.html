Privacy Policy – Dead Mile

Last updated: 26 October 2026

Dead Mile ("the game") is developed by HaStudios. This policy explains what information the game handles.

Information we collect

None. We (the developer) do not collect, store on any server, share or sell any personal or device information. The game has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no network features, and it does not request access to your contacts, location, camera, microphone or files.

Data stored on your device

Game progress (money, upgrades, unlocked vehicles and days, best distances and settings) is saved only on your device using the platform's local storage. It never leaves your device and is deleted when you uninstall the game.

Children

The game contains intense, realistic violence and gore and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

In-app purchases

The game contains no real-money purchases. All money is earned by playing.

Changes

If this policy changes (for example if a feature that handles data is added), the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
